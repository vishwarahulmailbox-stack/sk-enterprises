About us

From Passion to Perfection – The SK Enterprises Journey
What began as a spark of curiosity for Umesh Vishwakarma has grown into a trusted name in interior design. A self-taught visionary with over 22 years of hands-on experience, Umesh founded SK Enterprises in 2006 with a simple goal—to create spaces that feel personal, purposeful, and truly unforgettable.

Since then, we’ve transformed over 1,000 spaces—from cozy homes and elegant apartments to cutting-edge offices, boutique clinics, and luxury bungalows. Backed by a team of 150+ skilled craftsmen and a passionate crew of designers and project managers, we turn ideas into reality with unmatched precision and heart.

Whether you're starting fresh or reimagining your existing space, we’re here to co-create something that doesn’t just look beautiful—but feels like you.
