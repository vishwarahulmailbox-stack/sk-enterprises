About S K Enterprises

From passion
to perfection.

Established in 2006 by our founder, Umesh Vishwakarma, S K Enterprises has grown into a leading name in turnkey project solutions. With two decades of expertise, we specialize in bridging the gap between visionary design and seamless execution.

We pride ourselves on being a comprehensive partner for our clients. From initial conceptualization to final handover, we manage every facet of a project, ensuring quality, precision, and timely delivery — 350+ successfully completed projects and counting.

Whether it's a boutique clinic or a sprawling residential complex, we remain committed to transforming spaces through expert craftsmanship and reliable turnkey management.
